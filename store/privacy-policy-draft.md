# Mosslight Bastion privacy policy draft
Owner review and a real public policy URL are required before store release.

The game stores progression, preferences and queued optional achievement/leaderboard submissions locally on the device. The game code does not operate an account server, analytics endpoint, chat service or remote save server. Local game progress can be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling; export/backup is an owner/device responsibility.

On Android, the supplied Google Mobile Ads integration may contact Google's services to request ads and consent/privacy information. This test edition uses official test ad IDs. When configured and signed in, Google Play Games may receive the achievements and scores you submit. These third-party services have their own privacy policies and SDK data practices; the owner must verify actual dependency versions, complete Google Play Data safety and audience declarations, configure consent, and publish accurate contact information.

Advertising privacy options are reachable in Settings. Declining or lacking optional services leaves the ordinary game playable. No purchase or account is required by the game code. This draft is not a completed legal review or a claim that the SDKs collect no data.
