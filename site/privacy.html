Privacy draft

Owner review, contact information and a real public policy URL are required before release.

Game progress/preferences and queued optional scores/achievements are local. The game code operates no account, analytics or remote-save server. Android Google advertising and optional Play Games use third-party services with their own SDK practices. Advertising privacy options are in Settings; ordinary play stays available without those services.

See the complete store/privacy-policy-draft.md accompanying the source. The owner must verify actual dependency data practices, complete Google Play disclosures and provide contact/deletion information before publication.
