# Mosslight Bastion

Hold the lane. Keep the mosslight burning.

A complete portrait woodland defense game for Godot4.5.1 Compatibility:30 authored campaign missions, four complementary towers, six enemy roles, endless siege, date-seeded daily defense, keeper progression,20 local achievements and four local record categories. Plan at your pace with manual wave calls, free Rally and free ordinary retries. Preparation and cosmetic lantern upgrades use coins earned in ordinary play.

Open project.godot. Tap a build pad and inspect price/range before choosing a tower. Upgrade or sell a selected tower, call Next wave and use Rally when a cluster approaches. Back pauses combat. Optional native earned repair is limited once per run and leaves combat paused through the real fullscreen endcard until explicit Resume. Assisted runs retain ordinary rewards and cannot replace unassisted competitive bests.

Android is primary; single-threaded Web is supported by the export preset. Official Google test ad IDs and supplied native plugins are configured.
